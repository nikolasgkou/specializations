# 🧠 Artificial Intelligence
**Specialization branch · Zone01 Athens · 6 months**

> This branch aims to give a solid and practical understanding of machine learning algorithms and methodologies. It gives an overview of real life use cases in finance, image processing, natural language processing...

## The branch in numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Format | intensive piscine (~3–4 weeks) + 9 projects |
| Projects | 9 (4 solo · 5 team) |
| Core stack | Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras, SpaCy, Streamlit |

## What you'll build
- A real-time multi-stream YOLO person detector and counter using the supervision library, fine-tuned via transfer learning, quantized to ONNX, and served in Streamlit.
- A live webcam emotions detector pairing an OpenCV face pipeline with a Keras CNN that classifies seven facial emotions above a 60% test-accuracy threshold.
- A multi-language (5+ category, 2+ language) document classifier in TensorFlow with BERT/DistilBERT transfer learning and SpaCy NER tagging, audited at ≥85% accuracy and ≥5% above a TF-IDF baseline.
- An end-to-end S&P 500 quant strategy: leakage-free Bollinger, RSI, and MACD features, temporal cross-validation, an ML signal, and a backtest against the index.

## You'll learn
- Engineering features and validating models against measurable targets, starting with a 78.9% accuracy bar on the Kaggle Titanic leaderboard.
- Building classic ML pipelines with scikit-learn and pandas across structured datasets like Titanic and Home Credit.
- Training CNNs, BERT, and YOLO with TensorFlow/Keras, including transfer learning and ONNX quantization.
- Building a news-intelligence pipeline on 300+ scraped articles: SpaCy ORG NER, a >95%-test topic classifier, sentiment, and embedding-based environmental-scandal detection.
- Implementing PCA and SVD from scratch, with no sklearn or scipy shortcuts, for dimensionality reduction and clustering.
- Building recommenders on MovieLens, explaining models with SHAP, and shipping Streamlit and Dash dashboards.

## It's a great fit if…
- You like building end-to-end ML systems from raw data to a deployed dashboard.
- You enjoy hitting hard, measurable accuracy and AUC targets through tuning.
- You want breadth across classic ML, deep learning, NLP, and vision.
- You like implementing algorithms from scratch to understand their internals.
